# Section 10(13A) - House Rent Allowance (HRA)

## Overview
House Rent Allowance (HRA) is a component of salary provided to employees to meet their rental accommodation expenses. Under Section 10(13A), a portion of HRA is exempt from tax.

## Eligibility
- Must be a salaried employee receiving HRA as part of salary
- Must pay rent for residential accommodation
- The house should NOT be owned by the employee in the city of employment

## HRA Exemption Calculation
The exempt HRA is the **MINIMUM** of the following three:

1. **Actual HRA received** during the year

2. **Rent paid minus 10% of salary**
   - Salary = Basic + DA (if part of retirement benefits)

3. **50% of salary** (metro cities) or **40% of salary** (non-metro)
   - Metro cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata

## Formula
```
Exempt HRA = MIN(
    Actual HRA received,
    Rent paid - 10% of salary,
    50% or 40% of salary
)

Taxable HRA = Actual HRA - Exempt HRA
```

## Example Calculation
**Employee Details:**
- Basic Salary: ₹50,000/month
- HRA Received: ₹20,000/month
- Rent Paid: ₹22,000/month
- City: Bangalore (non-metro)

**Annual Calculation:**
- Annual Salary: ₹6,00,000
- Annual HRA: ₹2,40,000
- Annual Rent: ₹2,64,000

**Exemption (MINIMUM of):**
1. Actual HRA = ₹2,40,000
2. Rent - 10% salary = ₹2,64,000 - ₹60,000 = ₹2,04,000
3. 40% of salary = ₹2,40,000

**Exempt HRA = ₹2,04,000** (minimum)
**Taxable HRA = ₹2,40,000 - ₹2,04,000 = ₹36,000**

## Important Points
1. HRA exemption is NOT available in NEW tax regime
2. Cannot claim HRA and home loan interest for same property
3. If rent > ₹1,00,000/year, landlord's PAN is mandatory
4. If you own a house in a different city, you can still claim HRA
5. Rent agreement and receipts are required as proof

## Documents Required
- Rent receipts (monthly/quarterly)
- Rent agreement
- Landlord's PAN (if rent > ₹1,00,000/year)
- Form 12BB declaration to employer

## Section 80GG (Alternative)
If you don't receive HRA but pay rent:
- Deduction under Section 80GG
- You, spouse, or minor child should not own house in employment city
- Maximum: ₹5,000/month or 25% of income or Rent-10% income (whichever is lower)

## HRA vs Home Loan
- You CAN claim both if:
  - Home is in different city from employment
  - Home is under construction
  - Home is let out

- You CANNOT claim both if:
  - Living in your own house in same city
  - Home loan is for the house you're claiming HRA for
