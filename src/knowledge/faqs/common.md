# Frequently Asked Questions - Indian Income Tax

## General Questions

### Q: What is the difference between Assessment Year and Financial Year?
**A:** Financial Year (FY) is the year in which you earn income (April 1 to March 31). Assessment Year (AY) is the year following the FY in which you file your tax return and your income is assessed. For example, FY 2024-25 means April 1, 2024 to March 31, 2025, and the corresponding AY is 2025-26.

### Q: Which tax regime should I choose - New or Old?
**A:** It depends on your deductions:
- **Choose New Regime** if: You have minimal deductions (less than ₹3-4 lakhs) or your salary is below ₹7 lakhs
- **Choose Old Regime** if: You have significant deductions like HRA, 80C, 80D, home loan interest, etc.
- Use a tax calculator to compare both regimes for your specific situation.

### Q: What is the basic exemption limit?
**A:** For AY 2025-26:
- New Regime: ₹3,00,000 (first slab)
- Old Regime: ₹2,50,000 (for individuals below 60)
- Senior Citizens (60-80): ₹3,00,000 (old regime)
- Super Senior Citizens (80+): ₹5,00,000 (old regime)

### Q: What is Rebate under Section 87A?
**A:** Section 87A provides a rebate if your total income is below a threshold:
- New Regime: Full tax rebate if income ≤ ₹7,00,000
- Old Regime: Full tax rebate if income ≤ ₹5,00,000
This means if you're below these limits, you pay zero tax.

## Salary Related

### Q: What is Standard Deduction?
**A:** A flat deduction from salary income:
- New Regime: ₹75,000 (Budget 2024)
- Old Regime: ₹50,000
No proof required. Automatically applied.

### Q: Can I claim both HRA and home loan benefits?
**A:** Yes, in certain situations:
- If your house is in a different city from where you work
- If your house is under construction
- If your house is rented out
You CANNOT claim both for the same house you're living in.

### Q: How is leave encashment taxed?
**A:** 
- At retirement: Exempt up to ₹25,00,000
- During service: Fully taxable
- Government employees: Fully exempt at retirement

## Investment Related

### Q: What is the maximum I can invest in 80C?
**A:** The maximum deduction under 80C is ₹1,50,000. You can invest more, but deduction is limited to this amount. Includes PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, tuition fees, home loan principal, etc.

### Q: Is PPF interest taxable?
**A:** No, PPF interest is completely tax-free. It's one of the few EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) investments - contribution is deductible, interest is tax-free, and maturity is tax-free.

### Q: How are mutual fund gains taxed?
**A:** Depends on the type and holding period:
- **Equity funds (held < 1 year)**: 20% STCG
- **Equity funds (held ≥ 1 year)**: 12.5% LTCG above ₹1.25L
- **Debt funds**: Taxed at slab rate (no LTCG benefit after 2023)

### Q: What is the tax on Fixed Deposits?
**A:** FD interest is taxable at your slab rate. TDS is deducted if interest exceeds ₹40,000/year (₹50,000 for senior citizens).

## Deductions

### Q: Can I claim medical expenses as deduction?
**A:** Not directly, but:
- Health insurance premium: Section 80D
- Critical illness policy: Section 80D
- Medical treatment for specified diseases: Section 80DDB
- Treatment of disabled dependent: Section 80DD

### Q: Is education loan interest deductible?
**A:** Yes, under Section 80E. The entire interest (no limit) is deductible for 8 years from when you start repaying. Available for self, spouse, or children's higher education.

### Q: Are donations tax deductible?
**A:** Yes, under Section 80G:
- 100% deduction: PM Relief Fund, CM Relief Fund, specific funds
- 50% deduction: Approved charitable institutions
- Some donations have a 10% of income limit

## Filing

### Q: When is the ITR filing deadline?
**A:** For AY 2025-26:
- Individuals (non-audit): July 31, 2025
- Audit cases: October 31, 2025
- Belated return: December 31, 2025

### Q: Which ITR form should I use?
**A:**
- **ITR-1 (Sahaj)**: Salary, one house property, other sources up to ₹50L
- **ITR-2**: Capital gains, more than one house, foreign income
- **ITR-3**: Business/profession income
- **ITR-4 (Sugam)**: Presumptive business income (44AD/44ADA)

### Q: What happens if I miss the deadline?
**A:** 
- Belated return: Can file till Dec 31 with late fee (₹5,000 or ₹1,000)
- Updated return: Can file within 2 years with additional tax
- Interest: 1% per month on unpaid tax
