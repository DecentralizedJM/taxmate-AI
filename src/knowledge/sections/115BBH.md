# Section 115BBH - Virtual Digital Assets (Crypto/VDA)

## Overview
Section 115BBH was introduced in Budget 2022 to tax income from Virtual Digital Assets (VDA), including cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and other digital assets.

## What is a Virtual Digital Asset?
As per Section 2(47A):
- Any information, code, number, or token generated through cryptographic means
- Provides a digital representation of value
- Used as a medium of exchange, store of value, or unit of account
- Examples: Bitcoin, Ethereum, NFTs, tokens

## Tax Rate
- **Flat 30%** on gains from transfer of VDA
- No slab benefit - same rate for everyone
- Applicable regardless of holding period (no STCG/LTCG distinction)

## Key Provisions

### No Deductions Allowed
- Cost of acquisition is the ONLY deduction allowed
- No deduction for:
  - Infrastructure cost (mining equipment)
  - Electricity expenses
  - Transaction fees (gas fees)
  - Internet costs
  - Any other expense

### No Set-off of Losses
- Loss from VDA CANNOT be set off against:
  - Any other income (salary, business, etc.)
  - Gains from other VDA
- Loss from other sources CANNOT be set off against VDA gains

### No Carry Forward
- VDA losses cannot be carried forward to future years

## TDS under Section 194S
- **1% TDS** on transfer of VDA
- Threshold:
  - ₹50,000/year for specified persons (exchange)
  - ₹10,000/year for others
- Responsibility: Buyer/Exchange to deduct TDS

## Gift of VDA
- Gift of VDA is taxable in hands of receiver
- Fair market value on date of gift is taxable
- Exempt if received from relatives (as defined in IT Act)

## Example Calculation

**Transaction:**
- Bought Bitcoin: ₹5,00,000
- Sold Bitcoin: ₹8,00,000
- Transaction/Gas fees: ₹10,000

**Tax Calculation:**
- Sale value: ₹8,00,000
- Cost of acquisition: ₹5,00,000
- Gain: ₹3,00,000
- Tax @ 30%: ₹90,000
- Cess @ 4%: ₹3,600
- **Total Tax: ₹93,600**

Note: Transaction fees of ₹10,000 is NOT deductible.

## Multiple VDA Transactions

**Scenario:**
- Gain from Bitcoin: ₹2,00,000
- Loss from Ethereum: ₹1,50,000
- Gain from NFT: ₹50,000

**Tax:**
- Tax on Bitcoin: ₹60,000 (30%)
- Loss from Ethereum: Cannot set off
- Tax on NFT: ₹15,000 (30%)
- **Total Tax: ₹75,000 + Cess**

The ₹1,50,000 Ethereum loss is wasted - no benefit.

## Reporting in ITR
- Report under "Income from Other Sources"
- Schedule VDA in ITR
- Provide details of each transaction:
  - Date of acquisition
  - Date of transfer
  - Head under which income offered
  - Cost of acquisition
  - Consideration received

## Tax Planning Considerations
1. **Timing doesn't help** - Same rate regardless of holding period
2. **No loss harvesting benefit** - Losses cannot offset gains
3. **Keep records** - Maintain proof of acquisition cost
4. **Consider gifting** - To relatives might have some benefit
5. **TDS credit** - Ensure TDS is deducted and credited

## International Implications
- Income from VDA held abroad is also taxable
- Must be reported in ITR
- Foreign exchange rules apply

## Key Differences from Other Assets

| Aspect | Stocks/MF | Real Estate | VDA/Crypto |
|--------|-----------|-------------|------------|
| STCG Rate | 20% | Slab rate | 30% |
| LTCG Rate | 12.5% | 20% | 30% |
| Set-off | Yes | Yes | No |
| Deductions | Various | Various | Only cost |
