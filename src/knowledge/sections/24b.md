# Section 24(b) - Home Loan Interest Deduction

## Overview
Section 24(b) allows deduction of interest paid on home loan from income under the head "Income from House Property". This is one of the major tax benefits for homeowners.

## Deduction Limits

### Self-Occupied Property
| Condition | Maximum Deduction |
|-----------|------------------|
| Loan taken on or after 1.4.1999 for purchase/construction | ₹2,00,000 |
| Loan taken before 1.4.1999 | ₹30,000 |
| Construction completed within 5 years | ₹2,00,000 |
| Construction NOT completed within 5 years | ₹30,000 |

### Let-Out Property
- **Entire interest is deductible** - No upper limit
- Deduction from rental income
- Can result in loss from house property

### Deemed Let-Out
- If you own more than one property
- One property treated as self-occupied
- Others treated as deemed let-out

## Pre-construction Interest
- Interest paid during construction period
- Deductible in 5 equal installments
- Starting from the year of completion
- Part of the ₹2L limit for self-occupied

## Conditions for ₹2,00,000 Limit
1. Loan must be for purchase OR construction
2. Loan taken on or after 1 April 1999
3. Construction must be completed within 5 years from end of FY in which loan was taken
4. Lender must provide certificate of interest

## Section 80EE and 80EEA (Additional)

### Section 80EE (for loans sanctioned FY 2016-17)
- Additional ₹50,000 deduction
- Loan amount ≤ ₹35 lakhs
- Property value ≤ ₹50 lakhs
- First-time home buyer

### Section 80EEA (for loans sanctioned between 1.4.2019 to 31.3.2022)
- Additional ₹1,50,000 deduction
- Stamp duty value ≤ ₹45 lakhs
- First-time home buyer
- Should not own any residential property on loan sanction date

## Example Calculation

**Scenario: Self-Occupied Property**
- Home loan: ₹50,00,000
- Interest rate: 8.5%
- Annual interest: ₹4,25,000
- Pre-construction interest: ₹2,00,000

**Deduction:**
- Interest in current year: ₹2,00,000 (capped)
- Pre-construction (1/5th): ₹40,000
- **But total still capped at ₹2,00,000 for self-occupied**

**Scenario: Let-Out Property**
- Rental income: ₹3,00,000
- Interest paid: ₹4,00,000

**Calculation:**
- Gross rental income: ₹3,00,000
- Less: Standard deduction (30%): ₹90,000
- Net: ₹2,10,000
- Less: Interest u/s 24(b): ₹4,00,000
- **Loss from house property: ₹(1,90,000)**

This loss can be set off against salary income (max ₹2,00,000/year).

## Important Points
1. Available in OLD regime only (mostly)
2. New regime: Only for let-out property interest
3. Both co-owners can claim deduction proportionately
4. Must have possession of property
5. Loan from relatives also eligible (with proper documentation)

## Documents Required
- Home loan interest certificate from bank
- Possession certificate/letter
- Property registration documents
- Rent agreement (if let-out)

## Set-off of House Property Loss
- Loss from house property can be set off against other income
- Maximum ₹2,00,000 in a year
- Balance can be carried forward for 8 years
- Can only be set off against house property income in future
