# Section 80D - Health Insurance Premium

## Overview
Section 80D allows deduction for health insurance premium paid for self, family, and parents. This promotes health coverage and reduces out-of-pocket medical expenses.

## Deduction Limits (AY 2025-26)

### For Self, Spouse, and Dependent Children
| Category | Maximum Deduction |
|----------|------------------|
| Below 60 years | ₹25,000 |
| Senior Citizen (60+) | ₹50,000 |

### For Parents
| Category | Additional Deduction |
|----------|---------------------|
| Below 60 years | ₹25,000 |
| Senior Citizen (60+) | ₹50,000 |

### Total Maximum Deduction
- Non-senior + Non-senior parents: ₹25,000 + ₹25,000 = ₹50,000
- Non-senior + Senior parents: ₹25,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹75,000
- Senior + Senior parents: ₹50,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹1,00,000

## Preventive Health Check-up
- Additional deduction up to ₹5,000
- This is within the overall limit (not extra)
- Can be claimed for self, spouse, children, or parents
- Payment can be in cash

## What is Covered
1. **Health Insurance Premium** - Mediclaim, health policies
2. **Medical Expenditure for Senior Citizens** - If not covered by insurance
3. **Preventive Health Check-up** - Annual check-ups
4. **Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS)** - Contribution

## What is NOT Covered
- Premium paid for in-laws
- Premium paid for siblings (unless dependent)
- Service tax/GST on premium (debatable - check latest)

## Mode of Payment
- Premium must be paid by non-cash mode (cheque, card, online)
- Exception: Preventive health check-up can be paid in cash

## Important Points
1. Available in OLD tax regime only (not in new regime)
2. Policy should be in the name of taxpayer
3. Premium for married adult children not eligible
4. Group health insurance premium paid by employer is not deductible

## Documents Required
- Premium payment receipt
- Policy document
- Health check-up bills with receipt

## Example Calculation
Mr. A (45 years) pays:
- Health insurance for self and family: ₹20,000
- Health insurance for parents (both below 60): ₹18,000
- Preventive health check-up: ₹4,000

**Deduction:**
- Self + family: ₹20,000
- Parents: ₹18,000
- Health check-up: ₹4,000 (within limit)
- **Total: ₹42,000** (within overall limits)
