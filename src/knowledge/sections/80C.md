# Section 80C - Deductions for Investments

## Overview
Section 80C of the Income Tax Act allows individuals and HUFs to claim deductions for specified investments and expenses, up to a maximum of ₹1,50,000 per financial year.

## Maximum Limit
- **₹1,50,000** combined limit for 80C, 80CCC, and 80CCD(1)
- This is the aggregate limit, not separate for each section

## Eligible Investments

### Life Insurance
- Life insurance premium for self, spouse, or children
- Maximum deduction: Premium paid or ₹1,50,000 (whichever is lower)
- Premium should not exceed 10% of sum assured (for policies issued after 1.4.2012)

### Public Provident Fund (PPF)
- Long-term savings scheme with government guarantee
- Lock-in period: 15 years
- Interest is tax-free
- Maximum contribution: ₹1,50,000 per year

### Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
- Mandatory contribution by employees
- 12% of basic salary (employee's share)
- Employer also contributes 12%

### Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)
- Mutual funds with tax benefits
- Shortest lock-in among 80C investments: 3 years
- Potential for higher returns (equity exposure)
- No upper limit on investment, but deduction capped at 80C limit

### National Savings Certificate (NSC)
- Government savings scheme
- 5-year tenure
- Interest is taxable but eligible for 80C deduction

### Tax Saving Fixed Deposit
- 5-year lock-in period
- Interest is taxable
- Maximum deduction as per 80C limit

### Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- For girl child below 10 years
- Interest is tax-free
- Lock-in till girl attains 21 years

### Senior Citizens Savings Scheme (SCSS)
- For individuals above 60 years
- 5-year tenure
- Interest is taxable

### Home Loan Principal Repayment
- Principal portion of EMI for self-occupied property
- Only for new or under-construction property
- Registration and stamp duty also eligible

### Tuition Fees
- Fees paid for full-time education of children
- Maximum 2 children
- Only tuition fees (not donation, development fees)

## Important Points
1. Deduction available only in OLD tax regime
2. Investment must be made in the relevant financial year
3. Keep receipts and proofs for all investments
4. If policy is surrendered before maturity, deduction may be reversed

## Documents Required
- Investment receipts/certificates
- Premium payment receipts for insurance
- Home loan statement showing principal repayment
- Fee receipts for tuition fees
