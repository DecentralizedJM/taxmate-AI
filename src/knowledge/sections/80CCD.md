# Section 80CCD - National Pension System (NPS)

## Overview
Section 80CCD provides tax benefits for contributions to the National Pension System (NPS) and Atal Pension Yojana. It has three sub-sections with different limits.

## 80CCD(1) - Employee's Contribution
- Contribution by employee to NPS
- Maximum: 10% of salary (Basic + DA) for salaried
- Maximum: 20% of gross total income for self-employed
- **This is within the ₹1,50,000 limit of 80C**

## 80CCD(1B) - Additional Contribution
- **Additional deduction of ₹50,000**
- Over and above the 80C limit
- Available for both salaried and self-employed
- Total deduction possible: ₹1,50,000 (80C) + ₹50,000 (80CCD1B) = ₹2,00,000

## 80CCD(2) - Employer's Contribution
- Employer's contribution to employee's NPS account
- **No upper limit on deduction**
- Limit: 
  - 10% of salary for private sector
  - 14% of salary for central government employees
- **Available in BOTH Old and New tax regime**
- This is one of the few deductions available in new regime

## NPS Benefits Summary

| Section | Who Can Claim | Limit | Part of 80C? |
|---------|---------------|-------|--------------|
| 80CCD(1) | Employee contribution | 10% of salary | Yes |
| 80CCD(1B) | Additional voluntary | ₹50,000 | No (extra) |
| 80CCD(2) | Employer contribution | 10-14% of salary | No (extra) |

## Example Calculation
**Employee Details:**
- Basic + DA: ₹8,00,000/year
- Employee NPS contribution: ₹1,00,000
- Employer NPS contribution: ₹80,000

**Deductions:**
1. 80CCD(1): ₹80,000 (limited to 10% of salary) - within 80C limit
2. 80CCD(1B): ₹50,000 - additional (if contributed)
3. 80CCD(2): ₹80,000 - employer contribution (no limit issue as it's within 10%)

**Note:** If 80C is already exhausted (₹1.5L), 80CCD(1) won't give additional benefit. Use 80CCD(1B) for extra ₹50,000.

## Taxation at Withdrawal

### On Maturity (Age 60)
- 60% can be withdrawn lump sum - **Tax-free**
- 40% must be used to buy annuity - Pension is taxable

### Premature Withdrawal
- Minimum 80% to annuity
- 20% lump sum - Taxable

### Partial Withdrawal
- Allowed for specific purposes (education, illness, marriage, house)
- Maximum 25% of own contributions
- Can withdraw 3 times during subscription
- Tax-free

## New vs Old Regime
| Deduction | Old Regime | New Regime |
|-----------|------------|------------|
| 80CCD(1) | ✓ | ✗ |
| 80CCD(1B) | ✓ | ✗ |
| 80CCD(2) | ✓ | ✓ |

## Documents Required
- NPS account statement
- Contribution receipts
- Employer certificate (for 80CCD(2))

## Atal Pension Yojana (APY)
- Similar benefits under 80CCD
- For unorganized sector workers (18-40 years)
- Fixed pension of ₹1,000 to ₹5,000/month
